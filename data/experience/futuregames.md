A bit over two years of game programming at Futuregames in Malmö. It started with a lot of C# and Unity, then added C++ and Unreal, and in between there were a bunch of team projects where I got to make actual (small) games together with artists and designers.

## Game Projects

Every few months the whole class gets split into teams with artists and designers and we make a game in 4-7 weeks. These are the ones I'm the most happy with:

- **[Tamashii](/project.html?id=tamashii)** (Game Project 4, Unreal Engine 5, 11 people): a 2.5D co-op platformer where both players are tied together. I made the tether system and most of the character movement.
- **[Slaylien](/project.html?id=slaylien)** (Game Project 3, Unreal Engine 5, 11 people): a third-person shooter. I made the JSON-driven wave manager, the AI and swapping between three characters with automatic AI handover.
- **[Dead Sector](/project.html?id=dead_sector)** (Game Project 2, Unity, 16 people): a top-down sci-fi shooter where I did most of the UI and the quest system.

There was also a racing game where I ended up as a generalist and kind of unofficial lead. All of them are in the showreel below.

## C# and C++

The first course was C# in Unity, where we made a Vampire Survivors clone. After that the programme added C++ and Unreal Engine 5, which the later game projects were built in. C# and Unity are still what I use most for my own projects.
